## KEY RECOMMENDATIONS (Demo)

### Opportunity 1: Reduce DSO (Improve Collections)
- **Current**: 62 days | **Target**: 45 days
- **Action**: 
  - Negotiate faster payment terms with top 20 clients (10-15% of revenue)
  - Implement early-pay discount (2% for 10-day payment)
  - Introduce supply chain financing for large contracts
- **Impact**: ₹20-25 Cr cash freed
- **Timeline**: 60-90 days
- **Difficulty**: Easy

### Opportunity 2: Accelerate Growth & Market Expansion
- **Current Growth**: 42% YoY
- **Opportunity**: Expand into 2-3 new vertical markets (Finance, Healthcare, Retail)
- **Target Revenue**: ₹650 Cr in 2 years (2-year CAGR: 20%)
- **Investment**: ₹50 Cr capex + ₹30 Cr working capital
- **Expected ROI**: 35-40%
- **Difficulty**: Medium | **Timeline**: 24 months

### Opportunity 3: Improve Delivery Margins
- **Current Gross Margin**: 68%
- **Target**: 72% (industry best-in-class: 75%)
- **Actions**:
  - Automate 30% of service delivery (AI, RPA)
  - Increase offshore delivery mix from 40% → 60%
  - Improve utilization from 85% → 92%
- **Impact**: +400 bps margin = ₹18 Cr annual profit
- **Timeline**: 18 months
