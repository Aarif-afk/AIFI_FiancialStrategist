## POST 2: "The Operating Cycle Secret" (Educational)

**Headline:**
🚀 Why McKinsey charges $500k for this analysis (and how to do it yourself)

**Body:**

If you run a company or analyze businesses, you need to know this framework: **The Operating Cycle**

Most people use these metrics:
- Revenue ✓
- Profit ✓
- Cash Flow ✓

But they miss the **real driver of cash management**: The Operating Cycle

Here's how it works:

**Operating Cycle = DIO + DSO − DPO**

Where:
- **DIO** = Days Inventory Outstanding (how long cash is stuck in inventory)
- **DSO** = Days Sales Outstanding (how long it takes to collect from customers)
- **DPO** = Days Payable Outstanding (how long you wait to pay suppliers)

**Real Example: TechFlow Digital**

- DIO: 5 days (service firm, low inventory) ✓
- DSO: 62 days (enterprise contracts take time to pay)
- DPO: 35 days (pay suppliers quickly)

**CCC = 5 + 62 − 35 = 32 days**

Translation: **They need 32 days of cash to run operations.**

Now here's the power play:

**If they reduce DSO by 10 days: 32 → 22 days**
That means ₹15-20 Cr less cash required. Money freed up for growth.

**If they increase DPO by 5 days: 32 → 27 days**
Another ₹10-15 Cr freed.

**If they reduce DIO by 2 days: 32 → 30 days**
₹5 Cr freed (though DIO is already excellent for them).

**Total: ₹30-50 Cr working capital unlocked** without selling a single additional rupee of product.

This is why PE investors obsess over CCC. It's the difference between:
- 💰 A company that needs ₹100 Cr capital to grow
- 💰 A company that needs ₹60 Cr capital to grow

Same revenue, same margins, **totally different cash requirements.**

**Who else should know this?** Tag someone in your network who needs this mental model.

## The Operating Cycle: 8-Stage Diagnostic

**Stage 1: Capital Structure**

- Over-leveraged? 🔴

**Stage 2: People**
- Revenue per employee > ₹1 Cr? ✅

- Hiring efficiency deteriorating? 🔴

**Stage 3: Procurement**
- DIO (Days Inventory) vs industry benchmark
- Supplier concentration risk?
- Inventory turns improving? ✅

**Stage 4: Production**
- Gross Margin stable or improving?
- Gross Margin vs competitors?
- Operating leverage present? ✅

**Stage 5: Logistics**
- Project delivery on time?
- Warehousing efficient?
- Cost to deliver increasing? 🔴

**Stage 6: Marketing**
- Marketing spend as % of revenue?
- CAC (customer acquisition cost) vs LTV?
- ROI on marketing campaigns improving? ✅

**Stage 7: Sales**
- Revenue growth YoY
- Revenue quality (recurring vs one-time)
- Customer concentration risk

**Stage 8: Collections**
- DSO (Days Sales Outstanding) vs industry
- Collection rate > 95%?
- Bad debts increasing? 🔴

**The Master Metric:**
CCC = DIO + DSO − DPO

✅ Negative CCC: Excellent (paid before paying)
🟢 0-30 days: Good
🟡 30-60 days: Average
🔴 60+ days: Problem

- Value creation opportunities (₹10-100 Cr+)
