## Tips for Maximum Impact

1. **Authenticity:** Use real company examples (TechFlow, HIKAL)
2. **Specificity:** Always include numbers (₹25 Cr, 10 minutes, $0.50)
3. **Action:** Give people something to do (save, star, apply framework)
4. **Story:** Use narrative (the 3 questions, the ₹150 Cr discovery)
5. **Visual:** Add metrics tables, formulas, before/after
6. **Consistency:** Post every Monday & Wednesday (or Tue/Thu for dev)
7. **Engagement:** Reply to every comment within 2 hours first day
8. **Value:** Provide actionable takeaways (cheat sheet, playbook)

## FAQ

**Q: Should I post all content at once?**
A: No. Spread over 4 weeks (2 posts/week) for maximum reach and engagement.

**Q: What if a post gets low engagement?**
A: Repost 2 weeks later with different angle. LinkedIn algorithm favors consistency.

**Q: How long to see results?**
A: Week 1-2: Build awareness. Week 3-4: Engagement increases. Month 2: Consulting inquiries. Month 3: Brand recognition.

**Q: Can I modify the posts?**
A: Yes! Personalize company names, numbers, and examples to match your specific work.

**Q: What about GitHub repo when it's not live yet?**
A: Use "[Link coming soon]" in posts now. Update links when repo launches.
